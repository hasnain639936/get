Essential Guidelines

Important Information & Rules

Please review the essential guidelines to ensure a safe, hassle-free, and memorable ride.

Solo Rides Not Allowed Policy
In accordance with coastal safety regulations and open-sea conditions at Hawksbay and Turtle Beach, solo rides are strictly not permitted. Every Jet Ski must carry an accompanying companion or a certified Sahil Beach Hut instructor to assist in navigation, wave reading, and safety.
Advance Booking & Sunday Specials
Advance booking is strongly recommended, especially for weekends and Sundays. We offer special Sunday beach packages which bundle jet ski time with hut resting zones. Same-day walk-ins are subject to beach availability and tide conditions.
Fuel & Equipment Inclusions
All quoted prices for Standard, Premium, and VIP packages include 100% fuel. There are no surprise refuel fees. Life jackets, safety briefing, and ground support are fully covered.
Custom Locations & Transport
Our primary launch base is Hawksbay Beach and Turtle Beach Karachi. For custom private bookings at Mangrove Forest or exclusive private beach huts along the Sindh coast, trailer logistics and permit rates may vary slightly. Contact us on WhatsApp for tailored quotes.
