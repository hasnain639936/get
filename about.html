About Sahil Beach Hut

Built on Hawksbay Beach to make coastal adventure in Pakistan safer, clearer and better kept.

Our story

We started with one idea: you should enjoy the waves without worrying about hidden fuel costs, worn machines or safety. Every ski, hut and cabana is kept to that standard, and our instructors look after first-timers and experienced riders alike.

How we keep you safe

  • No solo rides. A partner or instructor rides with you.
  • Dedicated lifeguards on Premium and VIP.
  • Sanitized USCG-approved life jackets and kill-switch lanyards.
  • Fresh-water engine flush and steering check after every day.
