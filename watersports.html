Our jet skis

A maintained fleet of Yamaha WaveRunners and Sea-Doo craft, flushed with fresh water after every run.

Yamaha VX 1400 Sport

145 HP high-output engine, stable wave carving and a comfortable seat for up to 2 riders. A good first ski for families and couples.

Supercharged Rotax power with an Ergolock racing seat for rough water. Built for riders who want speed.
