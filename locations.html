Where we ride

Three spots on the Karachi coast. Need another location? Ask about trailer dispatch.

Hawksbay Beach

Rolling Arabian Sea surf, golden sand and beach huts. Best for speed and wave riding.

Turtle Beach

Clear turquoise water and calm reef breaks. Popular at sunset.

Mangrove Forest

Flat, glassy waterway with no swell. A quiet eco ride for beginners.
